🌧️ Rainwater & Mains Tariff

This tank is a software model; no rain gauge, mains valve, or pump is connected. Rain capture is estimated from live garden rainfall, roof area, and 80% collection efficiency. At 20% capacity, it waits while rain is active and simulates a mains top-up when current weather confirms dry conditions.

Default: £5.42/m³ England and Wales effective average estimate for 2026/27, calculated from the £639 average bill and typical household use. It includes fixed and wastewater costs, so it is not your supplier's exact marginal meter tariff. Replace it with your bill's volumetric rate for a closer estimate. Sources: Water UK, Environment Agency, ONS household size. Rainwater has no mains usage charge.
